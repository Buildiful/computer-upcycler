Privacy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Your device and guide

Browser-reported operating system and processor hints are processed on your device. Your selections remain in page memory, and PNG and PDF guides are generated locally. The tool does not read or write disks, install an operating system, or send your hardware selections, recovery keys, or guide choices as analytics event parameters.

Basic website analytics

On the official Buildiful GitHub Pages site, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and selected basic tool actions so we can improve the tool. We do not intentionally submit file contents, filenames, document text, or query parameters as custom analytics events. Our GA4 configuration disables advertising consent, Google Signals and ad personalization in the tag; associated Google Analytics property settings must also be checked separately.

When enabled, GA4 may use first-party cookies, including pseudonymous identifiers, and may process technical information about the browser and network from which an approximate location can be derived. See Google's Privacy Policy and GA4 cookies.

Regional privacy choices

In regions requiring consent for our analytics configuration, you can allow or decline analytics before Google Analytics loads. Where an accessible objection option is required, it is provided here. In other regions where basic analytics can lawfully run without those controls, it starts without a prompt. We honor supported browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling analytics. If regional classification fails, Google Analytics stays off until permission is given.

Regional check and local preferences

To determine the regional analytics experience, this page may contact IPWhois at https://ipwho.is/. That provider can receive the IP address associated with the request. Only a coarse consent category is saved locally for up to 12 hours. An explicit choice, when made, is saved in browser storage for up to 365 days and can be shared across tools hosted at the same origin. No location coordinates or full IP response are stored by this module.

Revoking consent or objecting stops later measurement from this module. Information previously collected by Google Analytics is not automatically deleted, and previously set cookies may remain until cleared or expired. Google Analytics data retention depends on the property's settings.

External downloads

Links lead to third-party websites. Their downloads and privacy practices are outside our control. Ubuntu is a trademark of Canonical Ltd. This independent guide is not endorsed by Canonical.

Other services and contact

This tool is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub and other delivery providers process ordinary network requests independently of Google Analytics. For privacy questions or applicable rights requests, use the contact options on Buildiful's website.
